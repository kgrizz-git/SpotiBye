- The pre-flight checklist and BT-3 disagree on the dev `ALLOWED_REDIRECT_URIS` value. Pre-flight only mentions `http://localhost:3000/callback,http://localhost:3000`, while BT-3 also requires `http://localhost:8080`. That makes the initial shared fixture incomplete for the later allowlist change.
- BT-7 says to keep `spotify_data?: unknown` for now and remove the field in a later plan, but the step heading says “Stop storing raw PII in KV session” and the bullet says “follow up by removing the field entirely.” That is internally inconsistent and could lead to a partial implementation without a clear end state.
- FT-1 instructs the method to raise `NotImplementedError` and still leaves behind an empty `results` dict “so the function still type-checks if any caller swallows the exception.” That is a weak contract for a public service method and should be clarified, because the current callers are also expected to catch and log the exception.
